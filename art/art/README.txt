ART WORLD — Website starter

FILES
- index.html: page structure and content
- styles.css: responsive premium editorial design
- script.js: mobile navigation, artwork filters, artist form and newsletter demo

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract art_world_website.zip.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.
4. Internet is needed for Google Fonts and the demo artwork images.

IMPORTANT
This is a front-end prototype. Artist submissions and newsletter entries are not sent or saved. Artwork names, artist names and prices are sample content. The links and artwork images are demo assets. Before launch, replace sample listings with approved artist content and connect a secure backend, storage, authentication, and payment provider.
